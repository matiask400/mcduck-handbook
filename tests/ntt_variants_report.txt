KACTL adaptations:
KACTL modulus configured to 998244353; duplicate declaration removed.
Root 62 -> 3 to preserve exact forward-transform values.
Minimum power-of-two padding, including s=1, preserves full 2^23 result limit.
Public vector<int> transform/inverse/convolution wrappers included in all measurements.

Recommendation: cached roots. Faster than baseline in all tested workloads; faster than adapted KACTL in 6/7 cold and warm cases.
Tradeoff: O(Nmax) persistent int root cache (about 2 MiB for a 2^19 transform; 32 MiB at 2^23).
A later small convolution retains the cache of the largest previous transform.
Asymptotics unchanged: O(N log N) convolution and O(N) memory; no support added for updates, negative coefficients or arbitrary moduli.
KACTL core alone is smaller/faster than its public adapters may suggest: int<->ll conversions are deliberately charged here.
KACTL uses vector<ll> roots and transform buffers plus bit-reversal array; our cached variant keeps int buffers and computes bit reversal in place.
KACTL tiny-batch advantage is small; no claim of a universal winner.
